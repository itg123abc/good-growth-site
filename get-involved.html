How we use your information

We use your inquiry to respond and understand regional interest. Form submissions are stored through Netlify, our website host. If we discuss regional demand with prospective partners, we share it in aggregate without your personal contact details. Email updates are optional; you can ask us to stop them or delete your inquiry by emailing ian@allgoodgrowth.com.

Having trouble with the form? Email ian@allgoodgrowth.com.
