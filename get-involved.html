What happens next

A conversation. A useful next step.

  1. Tell us your interest.

    Share where you are and what you want to explore.

  2. Find a practical fit.

    Talk through your operation, questions, and possible routes.

  3. Decide together.

    Agree on a useful next step. If that involves a trial, clarify costs, work, and responsibilities before it begins.
